Why Graph-Based?

Traditional recommendation systems use simple filters. Musico models real relationships between songs as a weighted graph — unlocking deeper, more accurate recommendations.

🕸️
Graph Data Structure

Songs are nodes in a weighted adjacency list graph. Edges represent similarity connections computed from audio features.

🔍
DFS Algorithm

Depth-First Search traverses the graph from a seed song, exploring deeply connected neighbors first — following the trail of similarity.

⚡
Weighted Edges

Each edge has a similarity score (0–1) calculated from genre match, BPM difference, energy level, and valence. Higher weight = explored first.

How It Works

From song selection to DFS traversal in 4 steps

1
Pick a Song

Search or browse our library of 50+ songs across 7 genres. Click any song to use it as your starting point.

2
Build the Graph

The backend loads all song relationships from MySQL and constructs an in-memory weighted adjacency list graph.

3
Run DFS

Starting from your song, DFS explores neighbors sorted by similarity weight — visiting the most similar songs first.

4
See Results

Your top 10 recommendations appear with a live graph visualization showing the exact traversal path taken.
